1. Overview

Ice Dice Entertainment (“we”, “our”, “us”) develops and publishes the mobile game Domino Sort (the “Game”). This Privacy Policy explains what information is collected when you play the Game, why it is collected, who it is shared with, and the choices available to you.

The Game has no user accounts, no login, no chat, and no in-app purchases. We do not ask you for your name, email address, phone number, or postal address, and we do not sell your personal information.

By playing the Game you agree to this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the Game.

2. Information collected

2.1 Information you give us directly

None is required to play. If you email us for support, we receive whatever you choose to put in that email (typically your email address and the contents of your message) and use it only to answer you.

2.2 Information stored only on your device

The Game saves your settings and progress locally on your device using the operating system’s standard app-preferences storage. This includes your current level number, sound and haptics settings, colour-vision mode, chosen language, and whether the tutorial has been shown. This data never leaves your device, is not readable by us, and is removed when you uninstall the Game.

2.3 Information collected automatically by third-party services

The Game includes third-party software development kits (SDKs) for analytics, crash reporting, and advertising. These SDKs may collect the following from your device:

  • Device and software information — device model, manufacturer, operating system and version, language, country/region, screen characteristics.
  • Advertising identifier — the resettable Google Advertising ID (AAID) used to select and measure ads.
  • IP address — used by our partners for coarse (country-level) location, fraud prevention, and ad delivery. We do not store your IP address ourselves.
  • App usage and gameplay events — app opens, session length, level started/completed/failed, moves made, deadlocks, ad offers shown and their outcome.
  • Crash and diagnostic data — stack traces, device state at the time of a crash, and app version, so we can fix bugs.
  • Ad interaction data — which ads were requested, shown, clicked, or rewarded.

We use this information in aggregate to understand how the Game is played, to fix crashes, to balance difficulty, and to support advertising. We do not attempt to identify you personally from it.

4. Advertising and your choices

The Game shows a banner advertisement at the bottom of the screen and an optional rewarded video that you may choose to watch to continue after a lost level. Rewarded video is never shown without your explicit action.

Ads may be personalised using your advertising identifier. You can control this at any time:

  • Android: Settings → Google → Ads, where you can reset your advertising ID or delete it entirely. Deleting the advertising ID stops personalised advertising across apps; ads will still be shown, but they will be non-personalised.
  • You may also opt out of interest-based advertising via youradchoices.com or networkadvertising.org/choices.

Because the Game is free and supported by advertising, ads cannot be disabled entirely.

5. Legal bases for processing (EEA/UK users)

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, or Switzerland, we process data on the following legal bases under the GDPR/UK GDPR:

  • Consent — for personalised advertising and for analytics, where consent is required. Where a consent request is presented to you, personalised advertising is served only if you agree, and you may withdraw consent at any time (see below).
  • Legitimate interests — for crash reporting, security, fraud prevention, and basic aggregated measurement needed to keep the Game working and improve it.

You have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict, or object to the processing of your personal data, and the right to data portability and to lodge a complaint with your local supervisory authority. To exercise any of these rights, or to withdraw consent, email nm.games.company@gmail.com — please include your device model and approximate date of play so our partners can locate the relevant records. You can also stop all further collection at any time by uninstalling the Game.

We do not maintain our own copy of this data or any database of users.
